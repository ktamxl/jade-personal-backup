### Thesis 2 — Jensen Huang: Sanctions Irrelevant

**The moat was never the chip. CUDA is the moat.**

## 💡 The BYD Parallel: Ken's Key Insight

China's domestic market is zero-sum. Once a company dominates at home, the government creates competitors to prevent monopoly pricing. So companies like BYD stop competing on price domestically and concentrate on exports. They make their real margins abroad.

**The DeepSeek question:** Will DeepSeek go the CUDA path (like BYD going global with exports) or stay Chinese-stack only (like a company trapped in domestic involution)?

If DeepSeek goes CUDA:
- Export market opens. Global revenue subsidizes R&D.
- DeepSeek becomes a global company.
- N2U (TSMC affordable tier) = physical vehicle to sell more chips globally while keeping China on older nodes.

If DeepSeek stays Chinese-stack only:
- China market only. Margin pressure from government-created competitors.
- Zhang Chi's thesis confirmed: structurally behind, staying behind.
- Involution ceiling applies to AI exactly as it applies to consumer tech.

**This is the critical test for DeepSeek R2.**
